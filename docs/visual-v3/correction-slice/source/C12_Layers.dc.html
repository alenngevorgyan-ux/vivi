The Correction · V3 vertical slice · board C12

Asset breakdown: seven layers, two of them static

Every location ships as a graphite plate and a paint plate drawn in register from the same camera. Everything that changes during play lives in the mask, the actors and the plates above them.

1 · Graphite plate
Static per location. Architecture and furniture drawn in pencil on paper. Furniture is paper-filled so it occludes.
2 · Paint plate
Static per location. Every surface fully painted with its local light. Never shown whole.
3 · Attention mask
Runtime. Soft-edged blobs around attention targets, torn by a noise texture. Reveals 2 over 1.
4 · Actors
Runtime. Rigged painted-mass figures at finish 0.25–1.0; hero with halo and prop; contact shadows; chair backs as occluders.
5 · Light & prop plates
Screen glow, daylight pools, phone/monitor glow, the slide and the summary as live-text plates above paint.
6 · Line + hung phrases
Beat line top-left; intention phrases with graphite leaders anchored to world points.
7 · Paper grain
One full-frame grain + mottle pass over everything, so plate, paint and actors share a surface.
