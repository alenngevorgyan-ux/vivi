Hallway: ten minutes outside the room

The break must earn its place. It is the only frame with daylight, the widest paper, the slowest clip, and the first time the player can hold the summary and read it without anyone in front of them.

01
Spatial relation
The meeting room is the glass wall on the right, its door 3 m away. The table, heads and screen glow stay visible in graphite through the glass.
02
Breathing room
Warm daylight bay, bench, plain wall. Calmer island, larger and softer edges. No corridor-shooter symmetry: camera offset left, vanishing point off-centre.
03
Stop and reconsider
Read pose: weight on one leg, head down, both hands on the pages. The summary is the hero object of this location.
04
Exit and re-entry
The same glass door is the portal both ways. Leaving (03a) and returning (04) use it; the player always knows where the room is.
