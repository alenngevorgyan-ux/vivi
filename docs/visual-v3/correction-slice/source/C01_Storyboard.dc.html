The Correction, start to reveal

Fifteen frames, eight beats. Desk → meeting → hallway → return → decision → enactment → boundary → author. Every frame is a production-target composition at 1920×1080.

The deck on the drive carries Mira’s name. The red line on the slide and on my printout bend at the same place.
02Meeting · presentationOBSERVE
Mira at the screen, the director at the table, me on the overflow chair by the glass. The slide is the brightest thing.
03aBreak · leavingMOVE
Through the glass door into the corridor, summary in hand. The chair by the glass stays empty.
03Hallway · breathing roomOBSERVE
Daylight, wider paper, the meeting still visible through the glass. I can stop and read my own pages.
04ReturnMOVE
Same room, ten minutes later: cups moved, one laptop shut. I stand inside the door instead of sitting.
05Primary decisionDECIDE
“Anything to add?” Three intentions hang on what they concern: my hand, Mira, the empty chair.

06cEnact · let it standACT
I sit, turn the summary face down and keep my hands on it. Silence is a confirmed act.

08aReveal · the page liftsREVEAL

08Author revealREVEAL

C
