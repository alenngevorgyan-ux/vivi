## What Vivi V3 is — locked behavior

- A human tension made inhabitable, followed by an encounter with the person/account behind it.
- An authored situation sequence; one scene can be enough, extra scenes must change understanding or the meaning of acting.
- Compile once/play many; no model connection during playback.
- Hand-authored gold inputs first, through the ordinary deterministic V3 path when implemented.
- Bounded attention, supported local movement, observation, reversible preparation and source-supported travel.
- One primary consequential act per launch experience; selection is cancellable and acceptance explicit/idempotent.
- Silence is a confirmed act about a named moment, never an inferred choice from idle time.
- Required knowledge comes from persistent fact receipts, equally available through observation and readable projection; no mandatory walking or hotspot clicking.
- Soft time by default; three golds have no real/hidden timers or reading penalties.
- Stable actors/objects with one owner/location each; returns retain facts, observations and consumed events.
- Exactly the hero’s named act is enacted; no generated historical response, counterfactual NPC reply or outcome branch.
- At acceptance NPC/causal/evidence progression freezes; hero-only enactment ends at the gold’s exact stop frame. Later hold/paint transition cannot extend the world.
- Actual author act/why/aftermath and option mapping are separately stored/released; never semantic inputs, scene evidence or public previews.
- Reveal preserves a physical trace, enters author act automatically, then reader-paced why/aftermath before continuation.
- Current V3 languages are **English and Russian only**. HY is future localization with no current fixture/font/screenshot/QA/human/generator work.
- Existing V1/V2 posts, routes, stored choice identities and Armenian support remain unchanged; V3 uses an explicit isolated loader path, no automatic migration.

All three gold accounts are **fictional editorial test stories**, never real submissions. This truth label applies in entry, readable mode, reveal, sharing/research material and any isolated prototype. The preceding lock list does not authorize production implementation or paid tools in this task.
