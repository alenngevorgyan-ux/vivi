Planning/specification deliverable · 2026-10-03 · parent planning commit `d035b4a4e8831a8dc687576d1f678e26d1ff72f9` · branch `prep/vivi-v3-format-proof`.

**Every account in this pack is FICTIONAL EDITORIAL TEST MATERIAL. None is a real user submission, an observed participant result, or evidence that the product works.** The fictional label must appear in the feed/hook, entry, readable mode, reveal and any research stimulus. The first-person author is an editorial character, not a fabricated account holder or testimonial.

This pack defines what to build and what to question before general generation. It contains three complete source-grounded experiences, fair controls, isolated semantic fixtures, a research protocol, ownership/dependency board and destructive review. It does not implement or connect V3, change V1/V2, generate art, call models, publish, or deploy. A contact sheet, clickable prototype and real-device/user evidence remain later work; the master plan’s complete Phase 0 gate has **not** passed merely because these files exist.

## Authority and separation

The final pre-boundary source is the only story evidence available to semantic authoring. The source pack numbers exact spans with NFC code-point offsets and hash. Editorially approved paraphrases are marked, not disguised as verbatim quotes. Private author act/why/aftermath have separate R spans and RF facts. They never become semantic inputs, scene captions, entity names, thumbnails, alt text, action hints or generator examples paired with a hidden “correct option.”

The three `*.semantic.json` files intentionally contain pre-boundary semantic content only. They are planning envelopes, **not** valid `PlaybackManifestV3` or a rival Foundation schema. A future adapter must validate claims against the private ledger, strip provenance as appropriate, derive approved kit geometry/schedules and load reveal separately. There are no executable recipes, coordinates, renderer imports, model calls or production registrations here.

Staging approval is separate from fact approval. Anonymous adult depictions, neutral practical light and approved doorway geometry can support a reconstruction without becoming observed facts. Blank numeric figures, placeholders such as “My name,” and optional neutral room beds are disclosed illustration. Identity, hostility, deadlines, facial motives, danger and private reactions cannot be invented as decoration. Final art requires blind inference review.

## Shared interaction contract

The complete V3 input/focus contract governs all three. Entry leaves page input native until the player intentionally focuses the named world surface. Physical WASD/arrows work only for a focused movement-capable world; native buttons, reading, dialogs, text, IME, blur and hidden tabs cannot move a body. Micro has no movement dependency. Enter opens an available target/action list; selecting an act opens confirmation; only a separate activation accepts. Held keys/repeated synthesized activation cannot accept twice. Tab can leave world controls; modal scope restores its invoker on close. Escape cancels an unaccepted intent/approach, never an accepted act.

Every required fact has a reader-paced visual and readable delivery at the same point in the spine. Required observations are not mandatory clicks: their checkpoint can deliver through Context/readable continuation with the same fact receipt. Optional fact inspection is never required. All options exist in the available-actions list regardless of proximity or mobile crop. A supported act recovers through approach/alternate mark/cut/readable enactment, never “too far.” Stage targets are native reachable controls with touch targets at least 44 CSS px; source text remains actual selectable/localizable text.

One explicit primary decision per attempt. Acceptance freezes NPC/causal progression, records once against decision revision, then performs only the hero’s named act. Silence is a confirmed act, never an idle inference. Cancel retains knowledge but writes no choice. Exit before acting leaves an incomplete attempt; “Read without playing” is explicitly nonparticipant. Skip after acceptance skips visual pacing only. Resume cannot reset consumed events or alter the accepted act. Missing assets use equivalent still/readable content; a reveal failure preserves acceptance with retry, not a substitute account.

## Shared presentation and reveal contract

Painted paper diorama, editorial frames, varied anonymous adult bodies, readable hero relation/prop at 390 CSS px. Kit geometry is Design’s authority; paths/camera bounds are derived by Foundation. No universal V2 horizon, procedural moral tint, random stare, soundtrack verdict, tiny figure maze or forced joystick. Build only the assets these stories need, with the master plan’s byte/decode and mobile acceptance targets.

Every event has `ambient`, `evidence_delivery`, or `causal` classification. No real timer or automatic act. Evidence and causal advancement pause during reading/selection/settings/modal/blur; ambient may continue neutrally where unobtrusive and stops on hidden/user pause. Quote receipts are one-shot; transcript reread is not a new utterance. Travel never spends decision time. A plateau is a supported held composition, not endless repetitive NPC activity.

The hero act stops at its explicit frame. Hold its physical trace, stop camera follow, then carry one motif onto paper. The first author act appears automatically; why and aftermath are reader-paced. No authored aftermath is reenacted as the player’s consequence. No textarea or comparison before the account. After account presentation, offer one honest continuation: another fictional experience, with a secondary private unsent related-story draft. This pack contains no counts, replies or sharing service. Reduced motion/mute/readable modes preserve all facts, intent, boundary and account order without animation locks. EN is authored; RU/HY variants require fluent source/meaning review and font specimens before parity is claimed.

No answer is inferred from dwell duration, control completion, equal choice distribution or fixture validation. General generation remains blocked until the master plan’s reviewed format/authoring gates pass.
