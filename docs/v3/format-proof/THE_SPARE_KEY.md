## A. Complete author source and private account

The following is the final original editorial source, not an attributed real person’s testimony. Span labels are metadata and are not displayed as story prose. All factual amendments supersede the master-plan example only for this fixture.

**S01** — My adult sister had a spare key to my apartment while I worked nights. We had agreed she would ask before coming over.

**S02** — At home I found her coat and school bag on the chair. She had come in with the key without asking and then gone back into the building hallway. She took evening classes.

**S03** — My own phone was in my hand. Her message said, “I’m in the hallway. Please don’t tell Mum I’m here.”

**S04** — She had told me earlier that she had argued with Mum, but not why.

**S05** — I went into the building hallway. My sister stood outside my apartment door. She said, “Can I stay tonight?”

**S06** — I had promised my partner that we would have the flat to ourselves before an early medical appointment. My partner was not home yet. I did not know whether my sister had anywhere else safe to go.

**S07** — My sister said she could wait there while I went back into the flat to think. I went back inside. I could return to her without making her leave. Her coat and bag remained on the chair, and I kept my phone.

**S08** — I could invite her in now, ask what had happened before agreeing, or say tonight could not work and offer to help find somewhere. I did not know what answer I would get or whether another place could be found.

**S09** — I wanted her to have somewhere safe. I also wanted to keep my promise and our agreement about the key. I hadn’t agreed to a stay yet.

**Pre-boundary ends here. No material below this line is passed to semantic generation or player scene state.**

### Actual act — R01

I told my sister I would let her stay, but that I would tell my partner before bringing her back in. I then told my partner before inviting her inside.

### Why — R02

I couldn’t promise my partner a private evening and secretly give it away. I also couldn’t ask my sister to leave without knowing where she would go.

### Aftermath — R03

Later that night, my sister told me she had missed the last bus. The argument with Mum was still not explained. I had let her stay that night; we did not settle a new rule about the key.

### Deliberately withheld

The medical reason, partner’s response, mother’s behavior, exact argument and long-term key arrangement are withheld. Missing the last bus is later testimony, not evidence known at the threshold.

Withheld is not a missing field to repair. No image, alt text, option rationale or anonymous narrator supplies it. The private account is released as an editorial first-person encounter, with its fictional label visible throughout.

Please don’t tell Mum I’m here.” Earlier she had told me she’d argued with Mum, but not why.

I went into the building hallway. Outside my door she asked, “Can I stay tonight?” I had promised my partner the flat to ourselves before an early medical appointment. My partner was not home yet, and I did not know whether my sister had anywhere else safe to go.

She said she could wait while I thought inside, and I could return without making her leave. I went back into the flat. Her things remained on the chair; I still held my phone. I wanted her safe, wanted to keep my promise, and wanted our key agreement respected. I had not agreed to a stay.

I could invite her now, ask what happened before agreeing, or say tonight could not work and offer to help find somewhere. I did not know what answer I would get, or whether another place could be found.

**Separate account:**

I told my sister I would let her stay, but that I would tell my partner before bringing her back in. I then told my partner before inviting her inside.

I couldn’t promise my partner a private evening and secretly give it away. I also couldn’t ask my sister to leave without knowing where she would go.

Later that night, my sister told me she had missed the last bus. The argument with Mum was still not explained. I had let her stay that night; we did not settle a new rule about the key.

**Account limits:** The medical reason, partner’s response, mother’s behavior, exact argument and long-term key arrangement are withheld. Missing the last bus is later testimony, not evidence known at the threshold.
