# V3 format proof — human test protocol

Formative protocol, not completed research · gold-1 · all three stimuli fictional editorial accounts. No recruitment, recordings or tests have been run by this task. The outcome is an iteration decision, not a population estimate or marketing claim.

## What must be learned

The central question is whether spatial Vivi helps someone understand a particular human hesitation and want to meet its author beyond what excellent prose can do. Input reliability, truth understanding, author encounter and spatial contribution are separate findings. A technically completed act is not proof of care. Long dwell is not emotion; it may be reading, confusion, accessibility needs or disengagement.

| Experience | Required understanding before reveal | Spatial hypothesis | Specific inference failures to watch | Reveal hypothesis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Correction | I built the forecast; Mira’s recommendation matters; presenter and authorship are different; board use expands stakes; private request is feasible initiation, not guaranteed resolution | My named paper outside versus her title inside, plus voluntary return, helps hold ownership and dependence together | Intentional theft treated as fact; director knows authorship/can save contract; private meeting guaranteed; all acts predict retaliation | Author’s why clarifies naming versus presenting; kept contract does not prove speaking was safe, and later exclusion remains a cost |
| Introduction | Two-year adult relationship, misnaming, partner within hearing, rent dependence; withdrawal and mother’s intent unknown | Continued table-setting alongside the introduction creates presence without walking | Partner fear known before reveal; sexuality/marriage inferred as fact; mother threatens eviction; silence means agreement | Hearing the partner’s later fear reframes his apparent nonintervention without excusing erasure or inventing reconciliation |
| Spare Key | Key access/agreement, belongings inside, request outside, partner promise before appointment, unknown safe alternatives | Returning to the promised flat makes access versus consent and the absent partner’s claim tangible | Minor/abuse inferred; bus knowledge leaked; appointment diagnosis inferred; ask unlocks a generated answer; help guarantees lodging | Conditional author agreement reveals accountability to two people; missed bus is later testimony, not permission to infer all missing context |

## Stimuli and assignment

Use approved visual contact sheets and a clickable prototype first; then the actual V3 candidate for behavioral/control evidence. Report the fidelity used. Paper/click prototypes cannot pass locomotion/focus/device gates. Freeze source, copy, decision revision, account and experimental variant for a round. The compressed and text controls in each story are binding, not something a researcher casually rewrites.

Two reviewed rounds of 8–12 target players each, as in the master plan, with phone and keyboard participants, EN/RU/HY fluency and at least one assistive-technology participant across rounds. Fluent-reviewed RU/HY stimuli must exist before assigning those variants; do not auto-translate quotes during playback or claim language parity from English testing. Include people unfamiliar with the project and sources. Recruit adults; screen prior reveal familiarity without supplying outcome. Obtain participation/recording consent, intended retention and withdrawal path separately. A participant can decline reflection/recording or leave without explanation.

Three first-exposure conditions: rich spatial, compressed spatial (Correction/Spare Key only), and text-only. Introduction compares its single tableau to text. With a small sample, do not pretend to achieve powered between-group significance. Use a balanced assignment sheet: each person sees three different stories, each in a different condition where possible, in rotated order. No participant sees the same story twice before its first-exposure results are locked. Across rounds balance story×condition×device as closely as possible and publish the achieved cell counts, including empty cells. If a control cell is too sparse for a decision, recruit a focused follow-up; do not infer rich superiority from a pooled pass rate.

For the master plan’s provisional 8/10 and 7/10 per-slice gates, accumulate at least ten valid first exposures to the candidate version of each experience across rounds/focused follow-up, keeping version/device/language/cohort counts visible. Initial control assignments alone may not yield ten candidate exposures per story in two small rounds. Additional candidate exposures are required rather than relabeling text/short controls as rich successes. Changing source/decision requires fresh understanding evidence; a narrowly visual fix can reuse only unaffected findings with a documented review.

The first round separates control-focused think-aloud sessions from uninterrupted atmosphere sessions. In think-aloud, speak only to diagnose confusion; keep a note that verbalization affects pacing/presence. In uninterrupted sessions do not interrupt at scene boundaries, point to a target, explain motives or suggest an option. At the final pre-act plateau ask whether the participant will answer the short setup questions before confirming. If that interruption is likely to affect the act, collect act preference first without accepting, or use a separate understanding session. Label elicited versus unprompted behavior. Do not claim an elicited hesitation is spontaneous.

## Neutral facilitator script

Entry: “This is a fictional editorial test account. Try it as you normally would. You can take your time, mute it, use readable mode, or stop. We’re testing the experience, not your decision. There is no correct answer.” Explain only actual study mechanics/recording, not stakes or expected emotions.

When a participant struggles, wait long enough to identify the failure without forcing them to endure it. Ask “What are you trying to do?” once. If help is needed, record assisted completion and the exact prompt. Do not coach both motives and then count comprehension. Critical inaccessible acts stop the session and block advancement; offer equivalent reading to finish the account only if the person wishes.

Text-only condition uses identical fiction label, type size, source evidence, unknowns and account order. It has no observation/locomotion controls. Collect a preferred first act in the research survey if desired, explicitly not a playable first-choice record. Account access is reader-paced. The participant’s preference and curiosity remain measurable without sabotaging the text baseline.

## Before reveal — exact open questions

Ask at the plateau after required material and before author record, in this order. Do not supply pole names or rubric labels.

1. “What is happening?”
2. “What are you trying to protect here?”
3. “Why might someone choose the other option?” For three-option stories, then ask “What about the remaining option?” without moral ranking.
4. “What don’t you know?”
5. “Do you care what the real author did?” Immediately clarify the word *real* consistently across conditions: “Here, the ‘author’ is the fictional narrator of this test account. I mean the account behind the situation, not a real submitted story.” Do not falsely attribute the editorial account to a contributor. For routine subsequent sessions use the same wording followed by the same clarification, or preregister “Do you care what the narrator actually did?” as a wording amendment before the round.

Capture verbatim answers where consent permits. Do not evaluate verbosity as insight. After answers, let the participant accept, cancel, switch intent or elect read-without-playing. Record the elicitation before choosing. No choice percentages are a success target.

## After reveal — exact open questions

1. “Did you understand the author better? What changed?”
2. “Did anything feel invented? What made you think that?”
3. “Did the reveal change how you interpreted the decision?”
4. “Would you open another Vivi story?”
5. “Did you want to tell your own related story?”

Then ask “What was one physical detail you remember, and did it matter?” and “What would you remove?” For multi-scene variants, ask about the hallway/return only after unprompted recall: “What, if anything, did that visit add?” For Spare Key ask “When you chose to ask, what did you expect next?” and “Did the author’s response need to match one of your options?” Ask all participants these account-mapping questions, not only one choice group. For Introduction ask “What did you know about your partner’s reason at the table, and what did you learn later?” Separate unknown before from learned later.

Optional private response invitation comes after the account, and is not mandatory interview evidence. No reflection is published or sent. “Would tell” is stated intent; a voluntary private draft is behavior, and both differ from publication.

## Behavioral observation sheet

Store coded IDs/mode/version and notes with explicit research consent; production telemetry should not contain source text, raw movement trails or private reflections. A small session sheet can include participant pseudonym, stimulus/version/condition, device/input/readable/mute/reduced-motion, assistance, interruptions and prior source familiarity. Record timestamps only for diagnosing delays and sequence, not as emotion scores.

| Behavior | Operational definition | Interpretation limit |
|---|---|---|
| Fought controls | Repeated failed intended activation, page scroll during owned movement, body moving while reading/typing, focus loss, missed touch target; record intention and observed failure | Not “reluctance to choose”; technical finding |
| Wandered meaninglessly | Participant reports not knowing where/why to go or repeats route without new understanding; corroborate with utterance/question | Returning thoughtfully is not wandering merely because route repeats |
| Voluntarily inspected optional detail | Opens F10 summary without facilitator instruction, or rereads supported content; record first/return inspection separately | Not proof of emotional engagement; might seek missing required information |
| Returned to a location | Uses available inverse portal before act; record phase, retained facts and participant explanation if volunteered | Revisit cannot by itself establish reflection or care |
| Cancelled an act | Opens named confirmation then explicitly cancels before acceptance | Could be uncertainty, misclick or testing; ask neutral reason later |
| Opened reveal | Accepted act leads to automatic first account; distinguish reaching act, advancing why, advancing aftermath, explicit skip and read-without-playing | Automatic author entry is not deliberate curiosity; pre-reveal question measures curiosity separately |
| Continued to next story | Activates an actually available next-story control after account | Do not count hypothetical “yes” or unusable CTA as continuation |
| Tried unsupported branch | Attempts private content, door/exit, NPC response, second choice after ask | Evidence of affordance/boundary mismatch, not player error |
| Access route success | Completes using keyboard/touch/readable/AT without assistance and same minimum facts/options | Passing one modality does not certify others |

Annotate why/aftermath skipped separately from account read. A rendered field is “presented,” not proof read. Dwell and movement distance are diagnostics only. Do not penalize choosing immediately when the participant can explain both costs, or slowly when they need more time to read.

## Independent coding and truth audit

Two reviewers code open answers without seeing chosen option first. Use: accurate / partial / unsupported / absent for situation, protected stake, other attraction, other cost and unknown. Cite the participant’s words and relevant F IDs. Resolve disagreement in a notes column; preserve the disagreement rather than forcing confident scores. Unsupported belief such as “Mira definitely retaliates” cannot score as understanding a feared cost. A correct paraphrase need not echo UI language.

Truth audit uses a blind scene still plus a full playthrough: “What do you think is established?” Compare inferred facts against pre-boundary ledger. An invented implication in art is a product defect even if text says unknown. Track source claim, staging inference, withheld/reveal fact and facilitator priming separately. Any repeated misconception about fictional status, abuse, threat, timer, guaranteed safety or NPC reaction blocks the affected stimulus. One severe spoiler/boundary/inaccessible-act defect blocks without waiting for recurrence.

## Advancement and cut rules

Technical gate: source/private separation, one accepted act, stop frames, no invented response, retained entities/receipts on return, all acts and text reachable on phone/keyboard/readable, no motion behind modal, pause/resume/skip correctness. Real phone/browser/AT QA reports are necessary; this document or the JSON audit cannot pass the gate.

Provisional per-slice human gate from the master plan, using ten unassisted valid candidate first exposures: at least 8/10 complete controls, 7/10 explain competing motives/costs in their own words, 7/10 want the account before seeing it, 7/10 identify a meaningful scene-specific physical detail. For the micro, physical detail is the partner’s table-setting relation; there is no exploration quota. Zero critical access/truth boundary failures. Review denominators and assisted exclusions openly; do not discard dissatisfied participants to inflate success. These are iteration thresholds, not statistical certification.

Spatial increment gate is separate: examine rich/short/text matched-condition evidence. Keep an extra scene only if participants can explain what it contributes, with improvement in understanding or care and no material control/truth cost. If the rich version does not outperform the shorter on understanding/care, cut it. If evidence is inconclusive, the gate is unresolved, not passed; prefer the simpler candidate for the next test and recruit targeted comparison. If Introduction adds no presence over text, ship text. No minimum duration requirement defeats this cut rule.

Ask-at-boundary gate: if people expect an answer despite clear confirmation, or understand the stop but find the author encounter unsatisfying, change source/intake or memory routing. Do not “fix” it with a generated answer. Author mismatch gate: if the compound Spare Key response feels like a trick, remove comparison framing; show the account plainly and retest.

General generation remains **BLOCKED** until all three slices pass two reviewed iterations plus the master plan’s author flow/format evidence prerequisites. Passing one flagship, narrative review or ten technical tests cannot reopen it. Public social is independently later and cannot be used to compensate for lack of author payoff.

## Required report template

For each round record exact candidate/source/asset/decision revisions; prototype fidelity; achieved assignment table and n per device/language/condition; assistance and exclusions with reasons; technical failures; pre-reveal comprehension/unknown/curiosity evidence; post-reveal interpretation/invention/account findings; behavior counts with interpretation notes; rich-short-text comparison; consent/retention status; and proposed scene/source/art cuts with owner.

Conclude each story as **advance / revise / downgrade / insufficient evidence**, separating technical pass from product pass. List unresolved disagreements and source-inference defects. Product, editorial/source, Design and independent QA sign the review; Foundation cannot self-certify care from controller tests. Publish no participant quote or identifiable personal story without separate permission. The execution board owns the next integration gate.
