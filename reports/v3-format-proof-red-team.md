## Decisions after destructive pass

Keep the required three flagship formats as **candidate stimuli**, with strong shorter/text controls. The Correction hallway and Spare Key return are explicitly vulnerable to deletion; their retention is conditional on spatial contribution. Introduction has no walking, observation chores or extra room. The author encounter is kept small and reader-paced. No option earns generated consequence, moral score, crowd agreement or a hypothetical response.

Every story now has explicit feasible initial acts and source-grounded unknowns. Correcting a real inequity is not “balanced” by pretending misattribution/misnaming is ethical; dependence makes silence understandable. The domestic situation is not repaired with invented abuse or guaranteed safe lodging. The ask option ends the counterfactual and states that plainly before acceptance.

## Reveal facts, human payoff and unknowns

All three R01/R02/R03 records are exact separately authored private fields, with separate RF facts. No outcome text enters public semantic projection. The offline check includes distinctive phrases; human spoiler audit still must inspect future alt text, assets, thumbnails, URLs, traces and author-option mapping.

Correction payoff: author’s acceptance of another presenter, refusal of authorship erasure, bodily vulnerability and later exclusion make the act personally costly. Contract retained and next update presentation are not causal evidence that courage was rewarded. Mira’s motive and recommendation remain unknown. The voice-shake and Mira not looking are private account text only, never NPC animation or player body affect borrowed backward.

Introduction payoff: the author wanted the partner to hear, and his later stated fear reinterprets the otherwise ambiguous table-setting. The mother changes subject in account only. No reconciliation, job/rent outcome, neighbor judgement or sexuality rationale is supplied. We removed any comparison highlighting as “author chose bravely”; the account is one person’s response.

Spare Key payoff: telling the partner first shows the narrator trying to honor both claims rather than “winning” a binary dilemma. Later bus testimony gives mundane context but does not explain the family argument or prove safety. A forced mapping to invite-now would falsely erase the condition/disclosure, so **no authorOption** is set. Partner’s response and medical reason are deliberately withheld. If users feel the account evades what they want to know, measure that; do not fill it with acceptance or conflict.

One spoiler caught in drafting: a proposed public UNKNOWN item named the “missed last bus.” Calling a future fact unknown still primes its answer. It was removed from all semantic facts/unknowns/controls and retained only in private account and editorial review material. The public unknown now concerns the unexplained need to stay, without supplying a specific cause.

## Is this simply text?

Possibly. All three have deliberately good prose controls. Correction’s additional value is hypothesized public/private credit contrast and chosen return, not walking time. Introduction’s is simultaneous table-setting while I am introduced, not the choice UI. Spare Key’s is physically occupying the promised flat with someone else’s things while permission remains outside, not extra source information.

All required/optional evidence and actual account are preserved in shorter/text versions. Rich loses if it adds no understanding/care or introduces confusion. Text wins if it communicates the relationship with equal presence and fewer control costs. We have not manufactured participant evidence or declared spatial superiority from editorial preference.

## Remaining questions / stop conditions

1. Will ordinary people care about the author’s choice in each specific situation before seeing it? Source specificity is promising but untested.
2. Does Correction hallway/return earn itself against the short control? If not, remove walkable hallway or compress entire format.
3. Does Introduction’s partner relation add presence over prose? If not, keep text; no new rooms to repair it.
4. Does Spare Key return inside clarify the promise without repetitive knowledge? If not, threshold-only control replaces it.
5. Does terminal ask feel satisfying and correctly expected? If not, revise source/intake or memory route, never answer it.
6. Does an unmapped compound author response feel human or evasive? Test plain account; do not invent comparison equivalence.
7. Are neutral geometry/poses actually neutral in finished art? Blind visual inference audit is pending, not passed by this ledger.
8. What local private response timing invites related stories without rushing the encounter? Account pacing/skip data and direct preference must be observed.

Repeated known-versus-unknown/fiction-versus-user/act-versus-response misunderstanding, one severe spoiler, or an inaccessible primary act blocks the stimulus. No dwell, vote balance or generalized mood score can clear that block. General generation remains blocked pending two reviewed iterations/all-three/authoring prerequisites.
